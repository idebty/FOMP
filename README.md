# Fancy Offline Music Player

A desktop music player designed to keep a local music collection organized, accessible, and easy to enjoy offline.

Whether you want to manage your music library, curate playlists, or keep everything in one place without relying on streaming services, Fancy Offline Music Player provides a simple and focused experience.

## Features

- Music library management
- Playlist creation and organization
- MP3 metadata support
- Cover art display and editing
- Shuffle, repeat, and volume controls
- Equalizer support
- Folder monitoring for automatic library updates
- Support for MP3 and WAV audio files
- Offline playback with no streaming dependency

## Why use it?

Fancy Offline Music Player is ideal for users who want:
- A local-first music experience
- A clean desktop interface
- Easy library organization
- Offline access to their collection
- Control over metadata and artwork
