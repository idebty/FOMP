Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Data collection

This app does not collect or store personal data on our servers. It has no analytics, advertising, or account system. Your music library, playlists, preferences, imported lyrics, and artwork are stored locally on your device.

When information is sent

Lyrics metadata is sent to LRCLIB when the app automatically looks up lyrics for a track and when you search or request suggestions. Discord activity and artwork are sent only when the optional integrations below are enabled and configured:

  • LRCLIB: When lyrics are looked up or suggestions are requested, the app sends relevant track metadata such as title, artist, album, and duration when available to LRCLIB. Requests are identified as Fancy Offline Music Player and are paced; the app honors LRCLIB Retry-After instructions when it returns 429 or 503. LRCLIB also receives your network request information, such as your IP address and the app’s client identifier. Lyrics you choose to save are stored locally. See LRCLIB API documentation.
  • Discord: If Discord Rich Presence is enabled, the app shares currently playing track details with the Discord desktop client. Discord may process or share that activity according to your Discord account settings and Discord’s privacy policy.
  • GitHub or Imgur artwork: If Discord Rich Presence and artwork sharing are enabled and configured, the app may upload track artwork to the selected provider so Discord can display it. GitHub uploads use the repository and token you configure; Imgur uploads use the configured client ID. Provider terms and privacy practices apply.

Your choices

Lyrics lookups require an internet connection and send the metadata needed to search. Discord presence and artwork sharing are optional and can be disabled in Settings. You can remove local library, playlist, and settings data using the controls in Settings.

Contact

This app does not operate a service that receives your library or account data. For LRCLIB API practices, contact LRCLIB through its official site.
